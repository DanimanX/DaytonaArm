DaytonaArm — Bring Your Own Data (BYOD)
=======================================

No copyrighted ROM files or ROM-derived compiled code are included in this package.

Required files on your handheld SD card:
1. Copy your legally dumped MAME ZIP ROM archive(s) into this folder (roms/):
     - roms/daytona.zip     (Daytona USA Revision A, 1994 — supports LAN Link Play)
     - roms/daytona93.zip   (Daytona USA Deluxe '93)
   Note: Repack .7z archives as uncompressed/deflate .zip before copying to the handheld.

2. Pre-compile the native ARM64 Cortex-A53 game module(s) on your PC using:
     tools/build-arm64-cache.sh /path/to/ports/daytonaarm cortex_a53
   Then copy the generated .so module(s) into cache/:
     - cache/daytona-aarch64.so
     - cache/daytona93-aarch64.so

3. First launch tip:
   Arcade factory defaults expect a linked twin cabinet. Open the pause menu
   (Start+Select or Menu), go to Options -> HOLD TEST BUTTON, resume to enter
   the arcade Test Menu, open GAME SYSTEM, and set LINK ID to SINGLE.
